# Authoring guides

These guides govern prose. Read this one first: it says which of them applies
to what you are writing, how they resolve against each other, and where they
yield to standards of your own.

They sit beside this file and refer to each other by bare filename.

## Which guide

Read `voice-base.md` whatever you are writing. It holds the rules the others
layer on, none of them repeats it, and skipping it means writing without most of
the rules. Then take the row below that matches.

| What you are writing | Read |
|---|---|
| a research or analysis report inside a code repository | `voice-business-technical.md` for how it sounds, `voice-research-report.md` for its reader journey, and `document-types-code-repo.md` for what it carries and in what order |
| other prose inside a code repository | `voice-business-technical.md` for how it sounds, and `document-types-code-repo.md` for what it carries and in what order |
| an essay, where persuasion is the job | `voice-professional-essay.md` |
| a grant application or a study protocol | `voice-scientific.md` |

The essay guide does not apply inside a repository. Do not reach for it because
the writing feels important.

Read the guide before drafting. Using one as a checklist afterwards produces
prose written one way and then patched, which reads worse than either.

## How they resolve

**A document-type obligation beats a voice preference.** What a document must
carry is not negotiable against how its sentences should sound.

## Where they yield

These guides are a default. They are not a claim on how you write.

**Voice is yours.** Where you bring your own standards for register, sentence
shape and word choice — in your own configuration, or given in this session —
follow yours. The voice guides here step aside, and you do not need to reconcile
them.

**Document shape is not.** What a document carries and in what order serves
whoever reads it next, not its author, so `document-types-code-repo.md` still
applies where your own rules have replaced the voice guides.

Where you bring nothing of your own, follow every guide in the table.

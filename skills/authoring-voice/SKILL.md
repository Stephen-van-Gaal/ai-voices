# Authoring Voice

**Read `authoring-guides.md` first.** It routes you to the guide that governs
what you are writing, states how the guides resolve against each other, and says
where they yield to standards you bring of your own.

Then read the guide it sends you to, whole. The guides are short, and skimming
for the rule you expect to find is how you miss the one you needed.
